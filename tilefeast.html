Who we are

Tile Feast ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game for Android published on Google Play by Raven Tobias ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, email olivolloyd26@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information or device information. It has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no in-app purchases.

Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following in your phone's private app storage (Android SharedPreferences):

  • your current level, total score, stars and best level score
  • how many boosters you have
  • your music, sound and vibration settings, and whether you have seen the tutorial

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time with Settings > Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this small file in your own Google backup; we have no access to it.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions and does not use the internet.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children under 13.

Third-party content

The food pictures come from Noto Emoji by Google and the font is Fredoka One, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The music and sound effects are original. All of these are packaged inside the app; no data is sent to their authors.
